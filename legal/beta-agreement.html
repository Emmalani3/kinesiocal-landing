KinesioCal Beta Tester Agreement & NDA (Draft)

1. Parties and Purpose

This Beta Tester Agreement and Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is between KinesioCal, LLC, an Indiana limited liability company ("KinesioCal"), and the business or individual named in the signature block ("Tester"). It takes effect on the date Tester signs ("Effective Date").

KinesioCal is developing a practice-management and booking platform for licensed massage therapists and other service businesses (the "Software"). Tester will use a pre-release version of the Software in its real practice, report problems, and give feedback, so that KinesioCal can improve the Software before public release (the "Beta Program").

The Software is unfinished. It will have bugs, may lose or corrupt data, and may change or be withdrawn at any time. Tester is joining the Beta Program with that understanding.

Tester's use of the Software is also subject to the KinesioCal Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Where this Agreement and the Terms of Service conflict, this Agreement controls during the Beta Program.

2. Confidentiality (NDA)

2.1 What is confidential. "Confidential Information" means everything about the Software and the Beta Program that is not public, including: the Software itself and its features, screens, workflows, data models, and pricing; roadmap and unreleased features; bugs, failures, and performance; documentation and build notes; the identity of other testers; and any communication with KinesioCal about the Software. Information is confidential whether shared in writing, verbally, on screen, or by giving Tester access.

2.2 What is not confidential. Information that (a) is or becomes public through no fault of Tester, (b) Tester already lawfully had before the Beta Program, (c) Tester receives from a third party with no duty of confidence, or (d) Tester independently develops without using Confidential Information. Tester's own client records and business data are Tester's, not KinesioCal's Confidential Information.

2.3 Tester's obligations. Tester will: use Confidential Information only to participate in the Beta Program; not disclose it to anyone other than Tester's own staff who need access to use the Software and who are bound by this Agreement; protect it with at least the care Tester uses for its own confidential business information; and not photograph, screen-record, or describe the Software publicly, including on social media, forums, review sites, or to competitors or other software vendors. Screenshots and recordings made for bug reports to KinesioCal are permitted and expected. These obligations bind Tester's owners and each individual staff member who is given access, personally and not only through Tester, and continue to bind each of them after they leave Tester or the Beta Program, including if they later work for or with another software company. Tester will inform each such person of this before granting access and will obtain their written agreement to be bound where KinesioCal requests it.

2.4 Compelled disclosure. If law requires Tester to disclose Confidential Information, Tester will notify KinesioCal promptly (where legally allowed) so KinesioCal can seek protection, and will disclose only what is required.

2.5 Duration. These confidentiality obligations last for the Beta Program and [3] years after it ends. Obligations covering trade secrets (including source code, data models, and the pricing and payroll logic) last for as long as the information remains a trade secret.

2.6 Return or destruction. When the Beta Program ends, Tester will delete or return all Confidential Information in its possession, except Tester's own exported business data, and will confirm in writing on request.

2.7 KinesioCal's obligations to Tester. KinesioCal will treat Tester's business data and client records as confidential under the Privacy Policy, and will not disclose Tester's participation to other testers without consent during the Beta Program; use of Tester's name in marketing is governed by Section 2.8.

2.8 Publicity. As part of the consideration for the Beta Program, Tester grants KinesioCal the right to identify Tester's business by name, city, and logo as a founding tester in KinesioCal's marketing, website, and announcements, during and after the Beta Program. Tester is not required to provide a review or testimonial; if Tester chooses to give one, KinesioCal may use it as given, with attribution to Tester's business, and will not edit it in a way that changes its meaning. Any testimonial must reflect Tester's honest opinion and experience. Tester may withdraw the right to use its name and logo by email after the Beta Program ends, effective for materials created after the withdrawal; materials already published may remain.

3. Protection of the Software

3.1 Ownership and license. KinesioCal owns the Software and everything in it. Tester receives a limited, revocable, non-transferable license to use the Software during the Beta Program for Tester's own practice. No other rights are granted.

3.2 No reverse engineering. Tester will not, and will not permit anyone else or use AI to: decompile, disassemble, or reverse engineer the Software; inspect, extract, or copy its source code, scripts, API responses, database structure, or embedded widget code beyond what ordinary use displays; probe, scan, or test its security; build or help build a competing product using knowledge of the Software's features or workflows; or remove any proprietary notice.

3.3 No AI ingestion. Tester will not paste, upload, or otherwise provide any part of the Software — including code, page source, API responses, error messages containing code, screenshots of administrative screens, or documentation — to any third-party artificial-intelligence or machine-learning service, including chatbots, coding assistants, or summarization tools, on any plan or setting. This applies regardless of the provider's retention or training policies. The reasons are practical: the Software's code, data models, and workflows are KinesioCal's trade secrets; screenshots, error messages, and API responses frequently contain Client names, contact details, or health information that Tester is not entitled to share; and code and system output reveal how the Software is built, which is information an attacker could use. Once content is sent to an outside AI service, neither Tester nor KinesioCal controls where it goes. Tester may use AI tools to draft its own bug reports from its own words, provided no Software content is included. This Section does not restrict anything Tester submits through KinesioCal's own support channels, bug-report forms, or in-app assistants: those go to KinesioCal, which may process them with AI tools as described in Section 5.4, and that processing is KinesioCal's responsibility, not Tester's.

3.4 Public statements during the Beta Program. Because the Software is unfinished, Tester agrees not to publish or post public reviews, ratings, comparisons, or derogatory statements about the Software or KinesioCal during the Beta Program, including on social media, review sites, professional forums, or to the press. Tester is encouraged to give KinesioCal blunt, honest, negative feedback directly; this clause restricts public statements only, and only during the Beta Program. Nothing here prevents Tester from making truthful statements required by law, reporting unlawful conduct, or speaking to Tester's own attorney. 3.5 Account access. Tester will not share login credentials or allow access by anyone outside Tester's business. Tester is responsible for all activity under its account.

4. Tester Responsibilities

4.1 Real use, good faith. Tester will use the Software in its actual practice for the duration of the Beta Program, try the features KinesioCal asks it to try, and give candid feedback.

4.2 Backups and data loss. Tester understands the Software may corrupt, lose, or make inaccessible any data entered into it. Tester is solely responsible for keeping its own current backup of all client records, appointment data, payroll data, and anything else it needs, outside the Software, at least [weekly] and before any update KinesioCal announces. Tester will use the export feature for this purpose where available. Pre-release software may also lose data through migrations or structural changes; KinesioCal will announce planned migrations in advance so Tester can export beforehand. KinesioCal has no liability for lost or corrupted data during the Beta Program, and Tester remains responsible for meeting its own licensing-board record-retention requirements from its own copies.

4.3 Bug reporting within 24 hours. Tester will report any bug, error, failure, data inconsistency, or security concern to [bug email / form] within 24 hours of noticing it. Each report should include, where possible: a screenshot or screen recording of the issue; what Tester was doing when it happened; the date, time, and page or feature; the device and browser used; and whether it can be reproduced. If Tester cannot capture a screenshot, a written description is still required within 24 hours.

4.4 Security issues. A suspected security problem (for example, seeing another business's data) must be reported immediately and must not be explored, repeated, or disclosed to anyone else.

4.5 Recorded troubleshooting sessions. Tester agrees that KinesioCal may schedule recorded voice calls, video calls, or screen-sharing sessions to reproduce and fix problems, and that those recordings may be kept by KinesioCal, transcribed, and processed with AI tools, solely for development and support. Tester will take reasonable steps to keep client health information off-screen during sessions, and KinesioCal will treat anything captured as confidential under the Privacy Policy. Tester's staff who join a session must be told it is recorded. [CONSULT: Illinois is an all-party-consent recording state; this clause provides consent for Tester, but each participant should still be told at the start of a call]

4.6 Lawful use and licensure. Tester represents that it and its practitioners hold the licenses and permits required for the services booked through the Software, and that it will not use the Software for any purpose prohibited by Section 3 of the Terms of Service. If Tester is a HIPAA covered entity (for example, because it bills insurance electronically), Tester will not store protected health information in the Software during the Beta Program unless and until KinesioCal has signed a Business Associate Agreement with Tester.

4.7 Clients. Tester is responsible for its own clients: for getting any consent needed to enter their information into pre-release software, for its own privacy notice to them, and for their experience with the booking portal. Tester's Clients are bound by the KinesioCal Client Terms of Use and Privacy Policy presented in the booking flow; Tester will not remove, hide, or alter those notices or the acceptance step, and will direct Clients to them when asked.

5. Support, Office Hours, and Communication

5.1 Limited support hours. KinesioCal is operated by its founder, who is also a working licensed massage therapist. Support is available during posted office hours, currently 7:00 PM to 12:00 AM Central Time, Sunday through Friday. No support is available on Saturdays. These hours may change; current hours will be posted at [support page / in-app] and Tester will be notified of changes by email. Messages received outside office hours will be handled during the next office-hours window.

5.2 Response targets. KinesioCal will aim to acknowledge bug reports within [2] business days and to acknowledge security reports within [6] hours during office hours. These are targets, not guarantees, and do not create any service-level obligation.

5.3 Email is the official channel. All notices, bug reports, and support requests go by email to [support email] or through [bug form]. Tester agrees to receive all communication from KinesioCal by email at the address in the signature block and will keep that address current. Text messages, social media, and personal phone numbers are not support channels. If additional channels become available they will be announced and posted in the updated Agreement.

5.4 AI-assisted support. Tester understands and agrees that KinesioCal uses AI agents and automated tools to triage, draft responses to, and in some cases resolve support requests and bug reports. Tester's reports and communications may be processed by those tools. A human reviews security reports, data-loss reports, and any matter Tester marks as urgent. Tester may ask for a human response on any ticket.

5.5 Emergencies. The Software is not an emergency service. If Tester cannot access the Software, Tester will operate from its own backups and records (Section 4.2) until service is restored.

6. Feedback, Fees, Disclaimers, and Term

6.1 Feedback. Tester grants KinesioCal a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free right to use any suggestion, idea, or feedback Tester provides, without attribution or payment. KinesioCal has no obligation to implement any feedback. Tester will not submit feedback it does not have the right to give.

6.2 Consideration and fees. In exchange for Tester's participation, feedback, bug reports, and the confidentiality and other obligations in this Agreement, KinesioCal provides the Software to Tester at no charge for the first 3 months of the Beta Program. After the first 3 months, KinesioCal may charge Tester a reduced beta fee to offset hosting costs, not to exceed 50% of the published price of the subscription tier Tester's account is on at the time, on at least 30 days' written notice before the first charge (so notice will be given no later than the end of month 2 if a fee will begin at month 4). KinesioCal may, at its sole discretion and at any time, extend the no-charge period, extend the Beta Program, or waive or reduce any beta fee, by written notice to Tester; such an extension or waiver is a courtesy, changes nothing else in this Agreement, and does not obligate KinesioCal to extend or waive again. When the Beta Program ends, KinesioCal will offer Tester a 50% founding-tester discount for 6 months on the plan Tester selects. Tester's account may carry a founding-tester badge, which KinesioCal may use in future promotions at its discretion; the badge itself confers no additional pricing or benefit unless KinesioCal announces one. Tester is not an employee or contractor of KinesioCal and receives no wages; the access and pricing described here are the full consideration for Tester's participation, and each party acknowledges that this exchange is adequate.

KINESIOCAL DOES NOT WARRANT THAT THE SOFTWARE WILL WORK, WILL BE AVAILABLE, WILL PRESERVE DATA, OR WILL CALCULATE PAYROLL, TAXES, OR TRAVEL TIME CORRECTLY. TESTER IS RESPONSIBLE FOR VERIFYING ANY PAYROLL OR TAX FIGURE BEFORE RELYING ON IT. TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, KINESIOCAL'S TOTAL LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT IS LIMITED TO THE FEES TESTER PAID FOR THE BETA PROGRAM, OR \$100 IF NONE, AND KINESIOCAL IS NOT LIABLE FOR LOST DATA, LOST PROFITS, OR INDIRECT OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES.

6.4 Remedies for breach of confidentiality. Tester acknowledges that a breach of Sections 2 or 3 would cause KinesioCal harm that money alone cannot repair, and agrees KinesioCal may seek an injunction without posting bond, in addition to any other remedy, including recovery of attorneys' fees. 6.5 Term and program guidelines. The Beta Program runs from the Effective Date until KinesioCal announces public release or ends the program, whichever is earlier, expected around March 31, 2027. Either party may end Tester's participation at any time on [7] days' written notice. KinesioCal may end it immediately for breach of Sections 2, 3, or 4.6, and may end it on notice if Tester's account shows no activity for [30] consecutive days. KinesioCal may publish and update practical program guidelines (such as the bug-report form, support hours, backup cadence, and which features are in scope for testing) by notice to Tester; guidelines supplement this Agreement and do not change its legal terms, and Tester may end its participation under this Section if it does not accept an update.

6.6 What happens at the end. If Tester continues onto a paid plan, its account and data carry over unchanged. If Tester does not continue, its account is treated as closed under Section 7.4 of the Terms of Service: portal access ends, Tester may obtain a complete export of its records by contacting support within 90 days, and after that KinesioCal may delete the data. During the Beta Program, records are subject to the same archiving and 30-month retention schedule as on the public Service. Sections 2, 3.1 through 3.3, 6.1, 6.3, 6.4, and 6.7 survive. Section 3.4 (public statements) ends when the Beta Program ends. If KinesioCal releases the Software publicly, Tester may continue on a paid plan under the Terms of Service and any founding-tester pricing in Section 6.2.

6.7 Disputes and general terms. This Agreement is governed by Indiana law. Disputes between Tester and KinesioCal are resolved under Section 14 of the KinesioCal Terms of Service (informal resolution, then binding individual arbitration in Lake County, Indiana, with the small-claims, injunctive-relief, non-payment, prohibited-use, and third-party-claim exceptions, class and jury waiver, prevailing-party fees, and one-year limitations period stated there), which Tester accepts by signing this Agreement. This Agreement is the entire agreement about the Beta Program, may be changed only in a writing signed by both parties, and may be signed electronically and in counterparts. Tester may not assign it. If any provision is unenforceable, the rest remains in effect.
