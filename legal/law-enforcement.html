1. Parties and Purpose

This Cooperation Agreement ("Agreement") is between KinesioCal, LLC, an Indiana limited liability company ("KinesioCal"), and the law enforcement agency or professional licensing board named in Section 2 ("Agency"). It is entered under Section 5.5 of the KinesioCal Terms of Service, which prohibits decoy or investigative accounts on the KinesioCal platform (the "Service") except by written agreement with KinesioCal.

This Agreement does not replace legal process. Disclosure of non-public Subscriber Data remains governed by Section 5.1 of the Terms of Service and requires a subpoena, warrant, court order, or statutory demand. This Agreement governs only the Agency's own activity on the Service: operating an account, placing bookings, or receiving ongoing cooperation during an investigation.

KinesioCal enters this Agreement to support lawful investigation of trafficking, sexual services, services involving minors, and unlicensed practice, while protecting its legitimate licensed Subscribers and the integrity of its records.

3. KinesioCal's Commitments and Limits

KinesioCal will:

  1. Provide the cooperation described in Section 2, and nothing beyond it, for the period stated.
  2. Flag any decoy account internally so that its own prohibited-use indicators, automated reporting, and termination rules do not act against the Agency's operation.
  3. Keep the existence and terms of this Agreement confidential, except to its attorney, except as required by law, and except to the minimum staff needed to carry it out.
  4. Preserve records relating to the matter for the term of this Agreement plus 90 days.
  5. Notify the Agency promptly if it learns the operation has been compromised or if a third party raises a complaint about the decoy account.

KinesioCal will not:

  1. Share any Subscriber's login credentials or administrative access with the Agency.
  2. Create, alter, backdate, or delete any Subscriber Data, or any record of a real Subscriber or Client, at the Agency's request. Records the Agency itself enters through a decoy account are the Agency's own entries.
  3. Give the Agency access to any Subscriber account, Client record, or data not named in Section 2.
  4. Make any representation to Subscribers, Clients, or the public about the decoy account's legitimacy beyond what the Service ordinarily displays.
  5. Disclose non-public Subscriber Data except under legal process per Section 5.1 of the Terms of Service.
  6. Allow the operation to target, book with, or interact with any licensed Subscriber in a way that interferes with that Subscriber's lawful business, except as specifically described in Section 2 and supported by the legal authority listed there.

KinesioCal makes no representation about the Service's reliability, the completeness of its records, or their admissibility. [CONSULT: chain-of-custody and records-authentication language if the Agency expects to use exports as evidence]

4. Agency's Commitments

The Agency represents and agrees that:

  1. Lawful authority. The operation is authorized under the legal authority listed in Section 2, is supervised by the official named there, and complies with applicable law and the Agency's own policies on undercover and online investigations.
  2. Scope. The Agency will use the Service only as described in Section 2 and will not expand the operation, add accounts, or target additional Subscribers without a written amendment.
  3. No harm to legitimate users. The Agency will not use the Service or any decoy account to solicit, induce, or pressure any person into conduct they would not otherwise engage in; will not book, cancel, or no-show appointments with licensed Subscribers in a way that costs them revenue, except as the operation strictly requires and the legal authority supports; and will not use access gained under this Agreement to investigate any Subscriber not named in Section 2.
  4. Record integrity. The Agency will not ask KinesioCal to alter, backdate, or delete records, and will not itself attempt to access, alter, or extract data outside the decoy account through the Service's interfaces, APIs, or widget code.
  5. Client data. Any real Client who books with a decoy account is a member of the public. The Agency will handle their information under its own legal obligations, will not retain it beyond the matter's needs, and will not treat booking an appointment, by itself, as evidence of wrongdoing.
  6. Indemnity. The Agency will, to the extent permitted by law, defend, indemnify, and hold harmless KinesioCal, LLC and its members from any claim, damage, or cost arising from the operation, including claims by Subscribers, Clients, or arrested persons, and claims that KinesioCal facilitated entrapment, defamation, or interference with business. [CONSULT: many agencies cannot agree to indemnify by statute or policy; counsel should advise on the fallback — a release of claims against KinesioCal, a hold-harmless limited to the Agency's own negligence, or declining to proceed]
  7. Credit and publicity. The Agency will not name KinesioCal in press releases, court filings, or public statements about the operation without KinesioCal's prior written consent, except as required by law or court rule.
  8. Fees. KinesioCal provides cooperation at no charge, except that the Agency will reimburse documented out-of-pocket costs (for example, engineering time to produce exports) at [\$___ per hour] if those exceed [4] hours. [decide]

5. Term, Withdrawal, and Signatures

Term. This Agreement runs from the start date in Section 2 to the end or review date there, not more than 90 days, and may be extended only by a written amendment signed by both parties.

Withdrawal. KinesioCal may withdraw from this Agreement on [48] hours' written notice to the official named in Section 2 if, in its judgment, the operation is harming legitimate Subscribers, exceeds the stated scope, or exposes KinesioCal to liability. On withdrawal or expiry, KinesioCal will terminate any decoy account and resume normal enforcement under its Terms of Service. Sections 3.3, 3.5, 4.5, 4.6, 4.7, and the confidentiality obligations survive.

Confidentiality. Each party will keep the existence and contents of this Agreement confidential except as required by law, court rule, or disclosure obligations in a prosecution. The Agency will give KinesioCal reasonable notice before any disclosure that identifies KinesioCal, where legally possible.

Governing law. Indiana law governs, to the extent not preempted by federal law or the law governing the Agency. [CONSULT: forum and sovereign-immunity issues when the counterparty is a government body]

Entire agreement; amendment. This is the parties' entire agreement about the matter in Section 2, may be amended only in a signed writing, and may be signed electronically and in counterparts.
