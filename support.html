Common questions

How fast will I hear back?

We aim to answer every email within one to two business days. Include as much detail as you can — screenshots help a lot.

Can I get my data out?

Yes — always. Businesses can export their client list and records, and clients can download their own history from their portal. If you ever need help with an export or want your data deleted, email us and we’ll take care of it.

How do I report a bug?

Email us with what you were trying to do, what happened instead, and the page you were on. Bugs from working therapists go to the top of the pile — the founder uses KinesioCal for her own practice every day.

Something about privacy or security?

If you believe you’ve found a security issue or have a privacy concern, email us with “Security” in the subject line and we’ll prioritize it. See our privacy policy for how we handle your information.

Still stuck?

Just email — there’s no wrong door. If we’re not the right people to fix it, we’ll point you to whoever is.
