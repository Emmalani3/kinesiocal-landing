What’s a calendar seat? A seat is one active therapist schedule — a bookable calendar column. Every subscription comes with one, and each additional therapist you activate adds a seat; deactivate any time. Logins are different: admins can create as many staff logins as they like — front desk, assistants, a bookkeeper — at no cost, on every plan. Getting paid is never gated.
