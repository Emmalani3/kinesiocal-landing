Add-on, any plan

Extra calendar seat

Another bookable therapist schedule — add one whenever you hire, on whichever plan you’re on. Tiers are for features; seats are optional.

$10/mo each

See what seats include
  • A full bookable calendar column for one more therapist, with their own schedule, services, hour caps and booking rules
  • Never jump a tier just to add a provider — seats work on Basic, Professional and Premium alike
  • Each seat brings 2 more free logins
  • Deactivate a seat any time; logins stay
  • Billed with your plan, prorated when it changes

What’s a calendar seat? A seat is one active therapist schedule — a bookable calendar column. Every subscription comes with one, and each additional therapist you activate adds a seat; deactivate any time. Logins are different: front desk, assistants, a bookkeeper — 5 logins come free with every plan, and each extra seat adds 2 more. Getting paid is never gated.
