Each feature exists because a working therapist needed it — here’s what KinesioCal does and why.

A calendar that works the way you do

Because your booking software should never tell you no on your own calendar.

Drag an open slot to book. Drag a card to reschedule. Each therapist gets their own column under every day, buffers and turnover time show honestly, and business hours shade the grid. All the rules you set for online booking — notice windows, spacing, day availability — shape what clients see. They never block you or your staff.

Screenshot: the drag-and-drop calendar with two provider columns

Standing appointments that don’t stop

Because regulars are the backbone of a practice.

Repeat any appointment every N days, weeks, or months — for a set number of visits, or forever. “Does not stop” books a full year ahead and quietly keeps extending itself. Move one visit or the whole series; cancel one or all; stop repeating from any appointment.

Screenshot: the repeat options with “does not stop”

Online booking you actually control

Because filling your book shouldn’t mean giving strangers the keys.

A copyable booking link with one toggle to turn public booking on or off. Set how much notice clients need, how far ahead they can book, which days each service is offered, and how tightly appointments pack. Guests can book without an account — and claim their history later if they make one.

Screenshot: the public booking page on a phone

Travel-aware mobile scheduling

Because a home-visit practice lives and dies by drive time.

For mobile sessions, KinesioCal checks real travel time between appointments before offering a slot, and pads your schedule so you’re never booked somewhere you can’t reach. House-call practices get honest schedules, not optimistic ones.

Screenshot: a mobile appointment with travel windows

A waitlist that fills cancellations for you

Because an empty slot should fill itself.

When a booked spot frees up, matching waitlist clients are offered it automatically, in order. Optional last-call blasts reach clients who asked for short-notice openings — and only them.

Screenshot: the waitlist page with a last-call toggle

Easy Mode

Because you became a therapist to do massage, not IT.

Pick Easy Mode at signup and you’ll only see the essentials: your info, your services, your hours, your calendar. Every other setting quietly uses a sensible default. Flip the toggle anytime to see everything — it never changes or resets a thing.

Screenshot: Easy Mode’s simplified settings

Why KinesioCal exists

KinesioCal was created by a massage therapist who runs her own practice on it every day. After years of using the big booking platforms, she found that every one of them was a trade-off: the one with the nice calendar couldn’t do invoices, the one with good reminders fought her on walk-ins, and all of them seemed to think the software knew her schedule better than she did.

She felt there needed to be a better platform for therapists and similar hands-on fields — one where the automation serves the practitioner instead of overruling them. So she built it.
