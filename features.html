Everything KinesioCal does

The complete list, grouped by what it does for your practice. Everything is included on every plan unless it carries a tag.

Calendar & scheduling

The master calendar

  • Day, week and month views with one column per therapist and a grid interval you choose (5 to 30 minutes).
  • Drag to book, drag to reschedule. Buffers, turnover and travel show honestly on the grid; business hours shade it.
  • Online rules never block staff. Notice windows, book-ahead limits, packing and caps shape only what clients see.
  • Staff can record the past. Create, edit and move appointments that already happened; only completed-and-paid visits lock.
  • Hard rules bind everyone: no double-booking, room capacity, provider hours.
  • Color by service or by provider, cancelled-appointment overlay, and a calendar key.
  • Walk-ins and “first available” round-robin, seniority, random or alphabetical — the owner picks how “any therapist” is assigned.

Availability & breaks

  • Business hours with multiple ranges per day and every-Nth-week repeats.
  • Per-provider weekly schedules; providers with none inherit the business hours.
  • Hours follow team availability (optional): public hours widen or shrink with recurring staff schedules.
  • Date exceptions for days off and custom hours; time-off requests can apply instantly or route to the owner.
  • Floating auto-breaks Pro: “30 minutes after 180 minutes of hands-on work”, visible on the calendar, repositioning as bookings land, locked a set number of hours ahead.
  • Manual breaks and time blocks on every plan.
  • Adjust-availability mode: drag on the calendar to add or remove hours instead of booking.

Standing appointments

  • Repeat every N days, weeks or months for a set number of visits or “does not stop” (books a rolling year ahead).
  • Just this one or the whole series is asked on every drag, edit and cancel.
  • Standing-slot suggestions Pro: when a client’s history shows a rhythm, KinesioCal proposes a series.
  • Deposits and prepay on series: every future visit asks for payment ahead via the reminder email, with a staff heads-up if it stays unpaid.

Scheduling optimizers Pro

  • Smart block-packing: public start times step by the whole service-plus-buffer block so no unbookable gaps form.
  • Public start interval: offer starts every 30, 60 or 90 minutes while staff keep the fine grid.
  • Consecutive-booking mode: inside a window, offer only times touching existing bookings.
  • Look-busy filter: hide a share of open public slots, deterministically per day.
  • Per-provider overrides for notice, consecutive mode and look-busy.
  • Duplicate-booking detector: warns when the same client books twice in a window.

Hands-on hour caps

  • Online hours cap per provider, per day and per week — counts only delivered service minutes, never buffers or travel. Every plan.
  • Physical-hours cap Pro: a stricter second cap for bodywork; consults and readings still book.
  • Cap-exception services Pro that never count and always book.
  • Staff can always book past a cap; they just see a warning.

Locations, rooms & equipment Premium

  • Multiple locations with their own address and time zone; the booking form gains a location picker.
  • Rooms and equipment as finite resources: a service that needs a treatment room or a hot-stone warmer only books while one is free.
  • Take a room out of service without deleting it.
  • Self-service offerings (sauna, cold plunge) with their own calendar column, capacity and automatic cleanup blocks.

Online booking

The public booking page

  • Your own link (kinesiocal.com/your-name) with one switch to turn public booking on or off.
  • Guests book without an account: service, time, name and email. Matched to existing records by email or phone, never duplicated.
  • Service-first or provider-first flow, with provider photos and a week or month calendar in the business’s time zone.
  • Your brand: logo, intro copy, primary and secondary colors theme the whole page.
  • Length variants, add-ons and “price varies” tiles; classes and events listed under the 1:1 services.
  • Jump to next available and a waitlist button when nothing suits.
  • Holiday-aware nudges Pro: a soft “Halloween falls on this date — still book?” that never blocks.

Booking policies & requests

  • Notice windows and book-ahead limits, business-wide or per provider.
  • Day availability per service (deep tissue only on Tuesdays and Thursdays).
  • Auto-accept or review: bookings land confirmed or as requests you accept or deny. Per-provider toggle.
  • Cancellation cutoffs and fees recorded as pending transactions.
  • Deposits and prepay in full at booking, with per-client overrides, gift-card and account credit applied first.
  • Abandoned-booking recovery: a declined card or closed tab reopens the slot and logs exactly where the client dropped out, with their details.

Restricted providers & the roster

  • Assigned-only providers: only their own clients can see or book them online — visible-not-bookable or hidden.
  • “Find your provider”: an existing client enters the email they book with and gets a confirmation link or code — no account needed.
  • Roster backfill and auto-assign on booking; providers can manage their own roster.
  • Roster-targeted waitlist and last-chance sends.

Intake & consent

  • Custom booking fields (health intake, how-found, anything) on every plan.
  • Consent and liability forms Pro: upload a PDF or start from policy, minor and prenatal templates; e-sign at booking or on paper; versioned with renewal tracking.
  • Targeted per service, class or event, with signatures stored on the client record.

Couples & pair services

  • One flow, two guests, two therapists, one time. Each seat checks real availability including buffers, breaks, travel and caps.
  • Linked appointments confirm, cancel and decline together.
  • Per-seat restricted-provider unlock using each guest’s own contact.

Find-a-provider directory

  • A public, searchable directory by location, name and specialty — every business is listed on every plan.
  • Profile page per practice linking straight to its booking page.
  • Fully mobile practices keep their address private while still anchoring travel times and near-me search.

House calls & mobile work

Mobile booking on every plan

  • Mobile-eligible services ask for a service address at booking (auto-filled from the client’s file).
  • Preset travel block per visit when you don’t need the engine.
  • Travel time by hand: override the drive for any single booking.
  • Mobile events reserve drive time to the venue and back.

Drive-time travel engine Premium

  • Real driving minutes per leg from a tiered routing engine (open-source routing first, paid live-traffic routing as a fallback).
  • Travel in and travel out stamped on each visit and drawn as bands on the calendar; shared client-to-client legs drawn once.
  • Safety margin, minimum per trip, and round-up to the grid.
  • “Find times that fit” lists bookable starts with the drive in and out on each one; the public page offers the same honest availability.
  • Bookend vs. isolated visits: suggestions snug against existing visits share a leg; otherwise each gets its own round trip. The awkward dead zone in between is never offered.
  • Neighbors recompute when a visit is created, moved or cancelled; anything that no longer fits gets a review flag.
  • Service areas fence public booking; staff see “beyond your area — you can still book it”.
  • Client address changes offer to move upcoming home visits and flag them for a travel re-check.
  • Mobile travel vs. billable time in Reports.

Services, classes & events

Services & pricing

  • Unlimited services, categories, length variants and add-ons with pre and post buffers and a color each.
  • Per-provider price and buffer overrides; opt a therapist out of a single length.
  • Flags: bookable online, mobile, physical (counts toward the hands-on cap), cap exception, couples, new-clients-only or existing-only.
  • Bulk CSV import of the whole menu — categories, services, add-ons, classes and events in one sheet, with a template.
  • Resource requirements per service and per add-on Premium.

Classes Pro

  • Reusable class templates with capacity, per-seat price, and public / private-link / offline visibility.
  • Each attendee is a real appointment: confirm, arrive, checkout, receipts and recommendations all work per seat.
  • Public sessions list with seats left; full or past sessions drop off by themselves.
  • Roster card on the calendar tile.

Events & chair-massage kits Premium

  • A window split into short sessions with turnover; the cap is computed from the window and the therapists.
  • Pooled or assigned chairs, per-therapist turnover, before and after buffers.
  • Public, private-link or paper-only booking; kiosk mode hands the tablet to the walk-up with the slot locked.
  • Flyer with QR code, blank and filled sign-in sheets with your disclaimer text; organizer gets them by email.
  • Organizer portal: the host sees the live sheet, moves participants, fixes a typo, downloads the sheets.
  • Who pays: each attendee or the organizer in full. Initials, no-shows and per-seat payment on the day.
  • Event waitlist when every session is taken.

Clients & safety

Client records

  • Searchable client list with quick add, appointment history, intake answers and payment history.
  • Client-visible and staff-only notes (with a standing no-PHI reminder).
  • Staff-only flags (good tipper, talker, needs extra time) with renewal and birthday reminders.
  • Per-client transition time: extra turnover for the client who needs it.
  • Merge duplicates and keep the other contact details as secondaries; every appointment, note, flag and package moves.
  • CSV import and export with a template, on every plan.
  • Contact edits with a review flow: staff edits to an account-holding client go out for the client to confirm; sign-in email changes verify at the new address.
  • Per-client payment rules: deposit or prepay overrides on the profile.

Blocklist

  • Block by client, email or phone with partial info; any filled contact point matches at booking.
  • Blocking means it: no online booking, no messaging you through the platform, no emails of any kind, roster assignments suspended.
  • Generic or custom refusal message; the reason and documentation stay internal.
  • Unblocking restores exactly what the block suspended.
  • Staff-requested blocks go to the owner for confirmation, with an interim block for that staff member only.
  • Review dates, documentation uploads and internal notes.

Shared safety network

  • Location-based and automatic: every participating business within your alert radius, no lists to manage.
  • Share a report from a blocklist entry, with or without documentation (undocumented reports are flagged everywhere).
  • Identity-minimized alerts: a match on a booking attempt shows the category and documentation status, never the identifier or the reporter.
  • Auto-import by category into your blocklist, or log only.
  • Platform-mediated challenges for the accused; minor-category reports expire after a year.
  • Never gated by plan. Launching after legal review.

Reminders, notifications & automation

Email reminders & follow-ups

  • A day-before reminder is set up from day one. Add more sends before and after the visit (2 per appointment on Basic, 3 on Professional, unlimited on Premium).
  • Post-visit follow-ups Pro.
  • Attendance confirmations Pro: a confirm-you’re-coming link whose ✓ shows on the calendar card.
  • Confirmations, cancellations and reschedules on every plan.
  • Sender identity: from-name and reply-to so replies reach you.
  • Email privacy toggle: strip service, provider and time from client emails and say “sign in to view”.
  • SMS reminders Premium when SMS ships.

In-app notifications

  • One bell and drawer for staff and for clients: requests, cancellations, client and team updates, safety alerts.
  • Per-category in-app and email toggles for each person.
  • Daily schedule digest Pro: each staff member’s morning rundown, with “No future appointment” next to the clients to rebook at checkout.

Waitlist & automations Pro

  • Waitlist by specific dates or “ongoing on Tuesdays”, with time windows and optional closed-hours enforcement.
  • Automatic opening offers in signup order when a matching spot frees up; manual blasts too.
  • Last-chance blasts for short-notice openings, only to clients who asked, with a link that unlocks times inside the notice window.
  • Auto-condense: consent-based move requests to tighten a gappy day, with owner escalation and a do-not-ask flag.
  • Client recapture: win-back emails to lapsed clients with dry runs, suppression and CAN-SPAM-compliant unsubscribe.
  • Recommendations library: private aftercare files and notes auto-sent after a service, delivered to the portal.
  • Milestone and care notifications.

Payments, commerce & money

Getting paid (never gated)

  • Stripe card payments on every plan: deposits and prepay at booking, card on file, pay links, refunds and account credit.
  • Checkout from the calendar with tips, sale lines, and pay-for-someone-else.
  • Unified point of sale for walk-in retail and standalone baskets.
  • Payments log of every attempt, decline, refund and saved card.
  • Invoices for any date range with itemized add-ons, paid status and provider credentials; staff email or download, clients request their own from the portal.

Gift cards, packages, promos & retail Pro

  • Gift cards composed from services, packages or cash, with an earmarked tip, sender and recipient emails, deliver-on dates and expiry you control (balances never auto-forfeit).
  • Packages and credits that redeem themselves at checkout.
  • Memberships: recurring dues that grant credits each cycle, auto-charged to a card on file, with portal pause, cancel and share per your policy.
  • Promo codes with constraints; retail products with inventory and images.
  • Online shop on the booking page for gift cards, packages and retail.
  • Redemption and refund terms per offering, printed on receipts and in the portal.

Money, reports & accounting

  • Money page: revenue by provider and period, commission splits, tips, earnings table with CSV export.
  • Reports: utilization, rebooking rate, no-shows and repeat offenders, revenue by service, mobile travel vs. billable time.
  • Accounting suite Premium: Schedule C categories, expense CSV import, reimbursement tickets, tax lines, W-2/1099 pay-run math, and a P&L with cash or earned toggle and the deferred-revenue line.
  • Deferred revenue done properly: gift and package sales post to a liability and recognize on delivery.

Team, portal & platform

Team & permissions

  • Free staff logins on every plan (5 per practice, plus 2 for each extra seat); you pay per active therapist schedule, never per login.
  • Roles plus per-person overrides enforced on the server; the UI hides what a member can’t use.
  • Provider profiles with photo, bio, calendar color, seniority rank, hour caps and license credentials.
  • Time off applied directly or through approval.
  • Activity, booking and email logs so “what happened?” always has an answer.

The client portal

  • One login across every practice a client visits: upcoming and past appointments, “I’m coming”, reschedule, cancel.
  • Your providers per business with one-click rebooking; forms, recommendations, cards on file, gift and package balances, memberships, transactions.
  • Waitlist joins with multiple dates and open-hours checks.
  • Contact edits that fan out to every linked practice.
  • Your data & invoices: download everything a business holds, request a PDF invoice, or delete with a 7-day grace window.
  • Hosted events for organizers.

Platform & trust

  • Easy Mode: essentials-only screens with sensible defaults; toggling never changes a setting.
  • Role-specific walkthroughs, in-app help docs and support tickets.
  • Google Calendar sync Pro per provider, two-way, with busy blocks reducing availability.
  • Light and dark mode, remembered on your account.
  • Data rights on every plan: client and business exports, anonymizing deletion with legal retention holds, every request logged.
  • Idle sign-out, encrypted sensitive fields, and audit trails built for a HIPAA posture.
  • Priority support and white-glove setup Premium.
