Coming soon

What we’re building next, in the order we expect to ship it. Dates are intentions, not promises — a working therapist sets the pace.

This year

Google Calendar connection

Two-way sync per provider: your personal calendar’s busy time reduces what clients can book, and KinesioCal appointments show up in Google.

Recurring spot finder

Ask for “every other Tuesday around 2” and get the standing slots that are genuinely free across the whole series, instead of checking week by week.

Next year

SOAP notes

Clinical charting with a body map, attached to the visit, kept separate from the booking record.

Health history intake forms

A proper intake — conditions, medications, contraindications — completed before the first visit and renewed on your schedule.

Travel safety network & safety monitor

For mobile therapists: a check-in timer for each house call, a trusted contact who is alerted when you don’t check out, and shared location safety across the network.

Google review integration

Ask happy clients for a Google review after the visit, from inside the follow-up you already send.

Square integration

Take payments through Square if that’s where your reader and your history already live.

Email newsletter editor

Write a newsletter in KinesioCal and send it to the clients who opted in, with the same unsubscribe rules as every promotional email.

Linked accounts

One sign-in across the businesses you work with or own — a therapist who rents at two studios, or an owner with more than one practice, switches without a second password.

Mileage tracking

Every house call already knows its drive; mileage tracking turns those legs into a per-trip log and a tax-ready total at year end.

No timeline yet

HIPAA certification

We already treat your data as if it were HIPAA-protected: encrypted sensitive fields, audit trails, idle sign-out, and access limited to the people who need it. The only piece we’re waiting on is the certification itself. We never sell client data.

Texting / SMS

Reminders and confirmations by text. Per-message costs and carrier rules make this one we’ll ship when it’s right, not when it’s rushed.

Payroll

Pay runs built from the commission splits, tips and hours KinesioCal already tracks — W-2 and 1099 alike. The math exists in the accounting suite; the payroll itself is a bigger commitment we’re not dating yet.
